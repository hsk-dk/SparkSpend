INSTRUCTIONS FOR CLEARING BROWSER CACHE AND RELOADING:

1. **Chrome/Edge/Brave**:
   - Press Ctrl+Shift+Delete (on Windows) or Cmd+Shift+Delete (on Mac)
   - Select "Cookies and other site data" and "Cached images and files"
   - Set time range to "All time"
   - Click "Clear data"
   - Then open http://yoursite and press Ctrl+Shift+R (hard reload)

2. **Firefox**:
   - Press Ctrl+Shift+Delete
   - Select all checkboxes
   - Click "Clear Now"
   - Then press Ctrl+Shift+R

3. **Safari**:
   - Develop menu → Empty Caches (or Cmd+Option+E)
   - Then Cmd+Shift+R

4. **If still not working**:
   - Close the browser completely (not just the tab)
   - Wait 10 seconds
   - Open browser again
   - Navigate to site fresh

The cache-busting parameter "?v=20260303" has been added to style.css
to force browsers to fetch the latest version of the CSS file.
